Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BEAR & SOW orders, dual-mode sizing, domestic delivery, and 7-day doorstep swaps.

How does the dual shopping mode (BEAR vs SOW) work? expand_more

BEAR is engineered for utilitarian, heavyweight men's training gear and oversized streetwear, while SOW is dedicated to sculpted feminine luxury athletic wear. Both identities live within the same ecosystem: you can switch modes at any time, browse complementary fits, and combine BEAR and SOW pieces inside a single shared cart with one unified checkout.

What are the shipping timelines across India? expand_more

We operate out of our central fulfillment hub in Bengaluru. All orders are packed and dispatched within 24 hours. Metros and Tier-1 cities receive air express transit in 2–3 business days; rest of India delivers within 3–5 business days across 19,000+ PIN codes with automated live WhatsApp tracking notifications.

How does 1-Tap WhatsApp Checkout work? expand_more

If you prefer skipping web forms, our verified WhatsApp gateway sends your cart breakdown and an instant UPI payment intent (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED). Once paid, confirmation and real-time tracking links are delivered directly to your chat window.

What is your return & exchange policy? expand_more

We offer a hassle-free 7-day doorstep swap policy. If the fit isn't spot on, initiate an exchange via our order portal or WhatsApp. Our courier partner picks up your item directly from your doorstep with zero return shipping surcharge, and replacement pieces are dispatched immediately upon pickup scan.

How do I choose my size for heavyweight interlock & compression fabrics? expand_more

BEAR t-shirts and hoodies are engineered with an intentional relaxed, oversized drop-shoulder cut using pre-shrunk 240+ GSM combed compact cotton. Take your true size for the intended streetwear drape, or size down for a standard athletic fit. SOW ribbed and contour pieces feature true compressive elasticity—consult our chest and waist centimeter charts for precise snugness.
